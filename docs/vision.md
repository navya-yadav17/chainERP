# Vision

chainERP aims to provide a blockchain-enabled ERP platform that improves supply-chain transparency and automates inter-organizational reconciliation.

## Primary objectives
- Reduce reconciliation friction between counterparties
- Provide immutable audit trails for critical business events
- Expose simple APIs for integration with existing systems

## Success metrics
- Decrease manual reconciliation time by X% (define X)
- Support N organizations and M transactions per minute (define N/M)
